MISSION

PICK YOUR BRO · LEFT/RIGHT TO BROWSE · FIRE TO LOCK IN

  • You do not choose your bro. One is assigned at random, and you are handed a new one every time you respawn or free a hostage.
  • Everything breaks. Shoot, blast or dig your way through dirt, wood and stone.
  • Press into a wall in mid-air to cling, then hold up to climb it.
  • Free the hostages — each one you touch adds a life to the shared pool.
  • One hit kills — yours, theirs, and your own explosives. Mind the blast radius.
  • Reach the extraction chopper at the far east end of the valley. Both bros must be aboard.
  • Gamepads work too: pad 1 → P1, pad 2 → P2. Esc pause · R restart · M mute.
